ACADEMIC PROJECT

ANALYSIS OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE:

Uses in Game Design Processes

Problem

Given the rapid evolution of AI worldwide, it has become necessary to understand how it might be applied to the field of video game development—a sector that constantly demands technological innovation and is currently highly profitable.

What I did

I analyzed four cases involving the use of AI in different areas of development, seeking to understand how they functioned and to examine their strengths and weaknesses. From this, I was able to deduce how such applications might influence the gaming industry in the future.

How I did it

I used published documents and articles that presented tests of these uses of AI.

Result

Of the 4 cases involving the use of AI, 2 stand out for their potential for broad industry application, even though there is still room for improvement (such as in the case of image upscaling technology, where coherence could be enhanced when increasing the resolution of a very small image).
